Learn AI. Build real things.

This sample syllabus outlines possible practical learning pathways for students and young professionals. It is a concept document, not a confirmed course brochure or enrolment contract.

AI Foundations · 4 weeks

AI in everyday life; prompting with purpose; research, source checks and responsible use; a guided project and presentation.

AI for Work & Automation · 6 weeks

Map routine tasks; prompt patterns; documents and knowledge; automation foundations; testing and human review; workflow demonstration.

Build with AI: Websites & Apps · 8 weeks

Find a problem; structure the experience; design with AI; build a responsive page; add interactions; use sample data; test and present.

Example tools

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Canva, Figma, Lovable, Make, n8n and Notion. Tool access, plan requirements and availability vary.

SAMPLE LEARNING OUTCOMES

Turn a brief into a responsive landing page and refine it.

04 · Analyse data with AI

Explore a dataset and communicate useful findings.

05 · Build a portfolio

Document your choices and present a project clearly.

06 · Use AI at work

Support research, writing, planning and analysis while checking outputs.
